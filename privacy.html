2. Location, specifically

Location is only used for the Distance widget, and only if you grant permission. If you don't use that feature, no location is ever collected.

  • We collect an approximate position (not high‑precision GPS) from your phone.
  • On Android and iOS, you may be asked to allow location "Always" rather than only while the app is open. This is so the Distance widget on your home screen can keep updating while the app is in the background or closed. You can decline this and use "While using the app" instead, or turn location off entirely, at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Your location is shared with exactly one person: the partner you're paired with. We do not use it for advertising, analytics, or anything else, and we do not sell it.
  • Turning location off stops new updates; it doesn't retroactively delete a distance value already shown to your partner, but no further location is collected or shared.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account, pair you with your partner, and keep the app's shared content (doodles, to‑dos, countdowns, affection, status) in sync between your two devices.
  • To calculate and show the distance between you and your partner, if you've enabled location.
  • To send push notifications about things your partner does (a doodle, a touch, an affection tap).
  • To process and verify a Distora Pro subscription, if you buy one.
  • To keep the service secure and diagnose problems.

We do not use your data to serve ads, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your paired partner. Distora's entire purpose is sharing certain content with one specific person you've chosen — that's not incidental, it's the feature.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf to run the app (listed in Section 5). They only receive what's needed to do their job and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • Legal reasons, if we're required to by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Distora, our users, or the public.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and content for as long as your account exists, so the app keeps working for you and your partner. If you delete your account (Section 7), your personal account data is deleted. Shared content already visible to your partner (like doodles you sent, or to‑do items on a shared list) may remain on your partner's side, since it's part of their history too — deleting your account doesn't reach into their copy of a shared list.

7. Deleting your data

You're always in control. In the app: go to Profile → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently:

  • Deletes your sign‑in account (email/Google), so you can no longer sign in as you.
  • Deletes your personal profile data.

It does not delete your partner's own copy of shared history (e.g. a doodle you sent them, or a shared to‑do list) — that remains on their side of the pairing, the same way a message you sent someone isn't erased from their inbox when you delete your own account elsewhere.

You can also request deletion, or ask any question about your data, without using the app — see our account‑deletion page or email us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

8. Children

Distora is intended for couples and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

All data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We use industry‑standard providers (Firebase, Cloudflare) for storage and infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.
