Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Distora account and personal data at any time, directly in the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Distora and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to Profile — tap your avatar or the profile icon from the Home screen.
  3. Scroll to "Delete Account" near the bottom of the Profile screen, under Account.
  4. Confirm when asked "Delete your account? This permanently deletes your account and personal data. This can't be undone."

That's it — deletion happens immediately, in the app, with no waiting period and no need to contact support.

Can't open the app (lost your phone, account is stuck signing in, etc.)? Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address or account you signed up with and we'll delete it for you within [X] business days.

What gets deleted, and what doesn't

DELETED IMMEDIATELY

  • Your sign‑in account (email/password or Google) — you can no longer sign in as you
  • Your display name and personal profile data
  • Your location data and distance‑sharing setting
  • Your push‑notification token
  • Your device status (battery/network) previously shown to your partner

KEPT (on your partner's side)

  • Doodles, to‑do items, countdowns and affection you sent while paired — the same way a message stays in someone else's inbox after you delete your own account
  • Your Distora Pro subscription itself is managed by Apple/Google and isn't cancelled by deleting your Distora account — cancel it separately in the App Store/Play Store if you don't want it to renew

We keep shared content on your partner's side because it's part of a shared experience the two of you built together, and deleting your account shouldn't reach into their account and erase their history. If you'd like that shared content removed too, ask your partner to delete the relevant item (doodle, task, countdown) from their side, or contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Not sure you want to delete everything?

If you just want to stop using Distora for now without losing your account, use Sign Out instead of Delete Account — your data stays exactly as it is, and you can sign back in any time.
